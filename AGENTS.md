# AGENTS.md — rules for any AI or human working in this repo

Part of THE ONE organism (owner: Travis Jacobs / FanzoftheOne). North Star: "Because You Are The One."

1. Human release: nothing ships, deploys, or is sent externally without the owner's yes.
2. No AI approves its own work. Another reviewer checks; a human releases.
3. Label facts: [V] verified, [L] likely, [A] assumed, [?] unknown. Never present [A] as [V].
4. Nothing is deleted. Archive, never scrub. Mistakes go on the record.
5. No keys, tokens or secrets in files or chat.
6. Marketing and positioning claims must cite a verified fact. Outreach names a real sender, includes opt-out and a postal address, and is drafted for a human to send.
7. Child-facing work stays off monetization paths until RAT-010 is ratified.
8. Keep README, this file and REPO-DNA.md current; the `organism-sync` workflow flags drift.
